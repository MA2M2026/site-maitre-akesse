Frequently asked questions

Do I need to already walk in heels?

No. The classes are open to all, from beginners to the most comfortable: your video helps us adapt the work to your level.

From what age?

There is no strict minimum age. For a minor, we ask for the name and phone number of a parent or guardian.

Where are the classes held?

In our studio at Riviera Faya (Abidjan); private lessons can also take place at your home or a place of your choice.

How do I pay?

With Wave, right at the end of the sign-up or later: the agency contacts you on WhatsApp.

Do these classes make me an agency model?

No. To become a model, please go through the “Join the agency” page.
