What we offer

Our Services.

Training talent. Building careers. Bringing projects to life. Academy, agency, artistic direction and production under one roof.

01

Academy

Learn the profession before claiming to practise it. Beginners and experienced profiles alike.

  • Recruitment

    Castings, candidate selection and identification of profiles matching the agency’s needs and professional opportunities.

  • Training

    Walk, posture, photo posing, attitude, presentation and professional conduct: training focused on the reality of the job.

  • Advanced training

    Support continues after the basics: experienced models keep working on their presence, image and level.

  • Coaching & masterclasses

    Sessions with industry professionals to broaden skills and understanding of the fashion and image professions.

  • Image consulting

    Style, presentation, book and comp card: presenting the right profile at the right time.

02

Agency & management

Once trained, a model builds a career: we handle representation, follow-up and development.

  • Career management

    Professional direction and goals, project selection, and the gradual building of a coherent career path.

  • Management

    Activity follow-up, assignment preparation, client coordination, negotiation and service follow-up.

  • Representation & placement

    Presenting models to clients and placing them with brands, agencies and event organisers.

  • Professional relations

    Ties with brands, designers, photographers and organisers: a network built around our models and their opportunities.

  • Communication

    Our models’ visibility and how their image is presented to the public, clients and brands.

03

For clients & projects

Have a project? We provide the talent and build the staging.

  • Models for your projects

    Events, runway shows, shows, campaigns, photo shoots and other services.

  • Casting & selection

    Searching for and selecting profiles to match the concept, needs and desired image.

  • Artistic direction

    Designing the visual and stage universe of an event or fashion project.

  • Fashion choreography

    Creating and rehearsing the models’ movements, passes and sequences for runway shows.

  • Runway shows & events

    Organising and supporting fashion shows, beauty pageants, shows and fashion events.

  • Image production

    Photo shoots, advertising campaigns and audiovisual productions related to fashion and modelling.

Depending on your project, the agency handles a single service or supports you at every step: casting, selection, artistic direction, choreography, production and staging.
