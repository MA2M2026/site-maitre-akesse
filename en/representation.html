Simple representation

You have at least two years of experience. The agency presents your profile to its clients in The Book, passes on requests and handles introductions. You do not belong to the agency: it is a simple, non-exclusive 2-year representation contract.

  1. Online applicationdetails, measurements, experience, recent photos, links to your work
  2. Reviewby the agency, remotely
  3. Representation contractsigned directly on the website
  4. Your appthe agency sends you the link: you publish your photos, your profile enters The Book, in the Main Board
  • Free: no fee, no training required.
  • You may work with other agencies.
  • Being in The Book does not guarantee a number of jobs.

Join the agency and be represented

Becoming an agency model means entrusting your career to the house, exclusively: introductions to clients and brands, castings, jobs, coaching and follow-up. Joining the agency is free.

To join the agency, you go through MA2M Academy, to be trained in modelling techniques and the standards of the house: 18 months if you are a beginner, a 9-month refresher course if you are already a model (the agency represents you from signature).
