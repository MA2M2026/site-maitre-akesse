Talent Network

Be represented by the agency

You are already a model.

You have at least two years of experience and would like to be introduced to our clients? Join the Talent Network. Your profile appears in The Book, our clients can book you, and the agency handles the introductions.

How it works
  1. Online applicationdetails, measurements, experience, recent photos, links to your work
  2. Reviewby the agency, remotely
  3. Representation contractsigned directly on the website
  4. The Bookyou publish your photos from your model space, in the Main Board
  • Everything is done remotely.
  • No training is required on this path.
  • Non-exclusive contract, for 2 years.
  • Being in The Book does not guarantee a number of jobs.

MA2M Academy

Train with MA2M Academy

You want to learn the profession.

You have never modelled, or you want to improve? MA2M Academy, the Academy of Modelling Arts, trains you for 18 months, in two 9-month levels: posture, walk, runway, photo posing, castings and shows.

How it works
  1. Programme and conditionson the MA2M Academy page
  2. Online applicationfrom your phone
  3. In-person castingwe get to know you
  4. Training contractsigned on the website, then classes from March to November
  • This is training, not entry into the agency: the training contract includes no representation.
  • At the end of the training, you are free. To join the agency afterwards, you apply separately, and the agency decides.

Agency Roster

Join the agency team

You want to build a career with us.

Joining the team of Maître Akesse Model Management means entrusting your career to the agency: introductions to clients and brands, castings, jobs, coaching and follow-up. The agency represents you exclusively. Every model on the team works to the Maître Akesse standards: that is why each of them goes through MA2M Academy.

How it works

You are a beginner

  1. Training18 months at MA2M Academy
  2. Assessmentby the agency, during and at the end of the training
  3. Selectionrepresentation contract, and you join the team

You are already a model

  1. Applicationwith your experience and portfolio
  2. In-person casting
  3. Contractthe agency represents you from signature
  4. Refresher courseduring your first 9 months, at MA2M Academy, to work to our standards
  • Joining the agency team is free.
  • The contract lasts 5 years and is signed directly on the website.
  • Training does not guarantee a place on the team: the agency decides according to its criteria.
  • The agency does not guarantee a number of jobs.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to pay to join the agency?

No. Applying, signing a representation contract and joining the agency team are free.

What is the difference between the Talent Network and the agency team?

The Talent Network welcomes experienced models, without exclusivity: the agency presents their profile and passes on client requests. The agency team (Agency Roster) receives full career management, exclusively.

I am already a model: do I need training?

For the Talent Network, no. To join the agency team, yes: a 9-month refresher course at MA2M Academy, while the agency already represents you.

Does the training get me into the agency?

No, not automatically. During and at the end of the training, the agency may select you to join its team.

Where do I sign my contract?

Directly on the website, from your space: you read it, download it and sign it online.
